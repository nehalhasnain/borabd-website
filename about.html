Who we are

About BORA

A zero-cost, project-based research training academy and its digital home at borabd.org — built on the premise that high-impact research requires attention and time, not capital.

The problem, and why now

Bangladesh has capable science graduates but lacks a structured, affordable bridge between finishing a degree and publishing research. Wet-lab research needs capital most students do not have. Dry-lab, computational, and epidemiological research — genomics, AMR surveillance, disease modelling, meta-analysis — needs only a laptop, an internet connection, and guidance.

The model is already proven. Remote, project-based, cohort-driven programmes have turned motivated students across the Global South into published computational scientists, entirely free to the learner, using open datasets, open-source tools, and volunteer mentorship. What is missing is a version scoped to Bangladesh’s own problems.

What is missing for Bangladesh specifically

  • Local relevance — no existing academy is scoped to Bangladesh’s One Health, AMR, and agricultural-export data problems.
  • No local archive — no equivalent open archive to showcase locally produced research output.
  • No feedback loop — global programmes are not designed to feed a Bangladesh-specific alumni-mentor pipeline.
  • The window is now — free AI-assisted tools have made self-directed technical training achievable at scale.

Leadership Note

A Message from the Founders

Research starts with questions. It does not cost money. It costs time and attention.

Md. Nehal Hasnain

Founder
“Scientific potential is evenly distributed, but opportunity is not. Across Bangladesh, thousands of brilliant students complete their degrees without ever formulating a research hypothesis or analyzing a real dataset. They are often told that meaningful research requires expensive laboratories and heavy funding. We founded BORA to break that myth. High-impact scientific inquiry begins with curiosity, open data, and rigorous mentorship.”

Rehan Hasnain

Founder
“True scientific progress occurs when tools, datasets, and knowledge are shared openly without paywalls or financial barriers. BORA is built on a non-negotiable principle: research education must remain completely free to every learner forever. By connecting motivated students directly with active mentors and peer-driven research pods, we let effort and dedication decide who gets to do science.”

Roadmap

Three moves

Months 0–3

Foundation

Domain secured; website MVP built; advisors and mentors recruited; curriculum and authorship policy finalised.

Months 3–6

Pilot cohort

First cohort on one track; run Stage 0–3 end to end.

Year 2+

Institutionalise

Open additional tracks; produce first publications; build national visibility.

Zero-cost sustainability

  • Train-the-trainer — no paid teaching staff; published alumni supply mentorship capacity.
  • Free-tier infrastructure — hosting, backend, and learning content run on free tiers.
  • In-kind participation — advisors and mentors are compensated in credit, authorship, and visibility rather than salary.

Risks we take seriously

  • Volunteer burnout — stage-gate filters for genuine motivation early.
  • Quality control — mandatory mentor sign-off plus plagiarism and data-integrity screening before submission.
  • Ethics — open or properly de-identified data only, with an ethics checklist for any human-health project.
Non-negotiable

Publication & authorship policy

BORA’s output is real research produced largely by students. A clear authorship and integrity policy is what makes journals, universities, and admissions committees take that output seriously.

Authorship order

Students who perform the core analysis and writing are listed as first authors. Core mentors and chief advisors are listed as senior or corresponding authors, reflecting supervisory contribution. Order is agreed at Stage 0, not argued at submission.

Review gate

Every manuscript requires sign-off from its supervising core mentor. Flagship outputs additionally require a chief advisor’s sign-off before any external submission.

Data & ethics

All analyses run on open or properly de-identified public data. A lightweight ethics checklist applies to any project touching human health records.

Integrity checks

Plagiarism screening and data-integrity verification are mandatory before any Stage 4 submission. No exceptions, including for mentor-led work.

Reproducibility requirement

Every published analysis ships with its code in a public GitHub repository and a documented environment. If it cannot be re-run, it does not go out under the BORA name.

Preprint first

We submit to a preprint server before or alongside journal submission, so the work is citable and visible immediately at zero cost.

Open by default

Where a journal offers a free open-access route, we take it. We do not pay article processing charges we cannot fund.

Closing

What we are actually claiming

BORA does not need grants, laboratories, or paid staff to change the shape of Bangladeshi science. It needs a clear pipeline, a handful of committed mentors, and students willing to put in the work. Every piece of this model has already been proven at global scale. Our task is to point it at Bangladesh’s own problems.
