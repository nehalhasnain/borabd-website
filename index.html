Why we exist

Mission & Vision

Mission

To democratize scientific discovery in Bangladesh by providing completely free, structured, and project-based research training to undergraduate talent. We pair eager students with experienced mentors to produce high-impact, peer-reviewed science without financial barriers.

Vision

To build Bangladesh's largest, self-sustaining network of open-access researchers. We aim to prove to the global scientific community that world-class research requires curiosity, time, and structured guidance, not massive financial capital.
Leadership Note

A Message from the Founders

Research starts with questions. It does not cost money. It costs time and attention.

Md. Nehal Hasnain

Founder
“Scientific potential is evenly distributed, but opportunity is not. Across Bangladesh, thousands of brilliant students complete their degrees without ever formulating a research hypothesis or analyzing a real dataset. They are often told that meaningful research requires expensive laboratories and heavy funding. We founded BORA to break that myth. High-impact scientific inquiry begins with curiosity, open data, and rigorous mentorship.”

Rehan Hasnain

Founder
“True scientific progress occurs when tools, datasets, and knowledge are shared openly without paywalls or financial barriers. BORA is built on a non-negotiable principle: research education must remain completely free to every learner forever. By connecting motivated students directly with active mentors and peer-driven research pods, we let effort and dedication decide who gets to do science.”

How we think

Core Philosophy

Knowledge must remain free and open. By removing the financial barrier to training, we let capability — not funding — decide who gets to do science.

01

Free Access

Every learning resource, tool, and dataset BORA uses must be free and openly licensed. No fees, no paywalls, no hidden costs to any learner — ever.

02

Peer-Powered Mentorship

Every published researcher is expected to mentor the next cohort. Teaching capacity grows with output instead of requiring paid staff.

03

Publish, Don’t Just Learn

Every cohort works toward a real, defensible analysis or manuscript — not tutorials, not certificates of attendance.
