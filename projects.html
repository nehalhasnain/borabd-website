Our Projects

Every BORA project is chosen on one core test: can a motivated student with a laptop or open-access field protocol produce a defensible, publishable result? If yes, it becomes a BORA project.

Research Capabilities

Two Execution Pathways

BORA operates across computational and field surveillance environments to deliver institutional-grade research with zero budget barriers.

💻 Dry Lab (Computational & Data-Driven)

Leveraging Python, R, data science frameworks, and advanced AI APIs to conduct high-scale research with zero lab overhead. Focuses on bioinformatics, public health modeling, genomic surveillance, and literature meta-analysis using open repositories (NCBI SRA, ENA, GISAID).

🔬 Wet Lab & Field Surveillance

Utilizing low-cost sample collection (active field surveillance, market sampling) and strategic laboratory partnerships across university or independent spaces. Applied to One Health, veterinary microbiology, food safety, and clinical biomedicine.

Track 1

One Health & Public Health

Zoonotic Spillover Risk Surface for Bangladesh

Combining livestock density, land-use change, and reported human case data to build a district-level spillover risk mapping index.

R / Python Spatial GIS Epidemiology

Infectious Disease Epidemiology

Flagship Dry Lab Enrolling Cohort 1

Outbreak Trend & Seasonality Modeling — Dengue & Measles

AMR & Genomic Surveillance

Mining public SRA and ENA sequence repositories for Bangladeshi bacterial isolates, executing assembly, and mapping resistance gene profiles.

Omics & Bioinformatics
